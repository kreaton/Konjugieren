## Fonts, data and online services

The app uses the system fonts of the device (Segoe UI, Georgia and their fallbacks); no web fonts are loaded and none are included in this repository. The app makes no calls to online services at run time apart from loading its own files from GitHub Pages. Verb pages link to Wiktionary.
